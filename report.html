Report a shared roster

On the Roster community, press Report on the roster's card. You need to be signed in. Rosters with several reports are hidden automatically and we review them.

Copyright and removal requests

If something on 2UHub infringes your copyright or other rights, or you are the owner of a name, image or design shown here and want it removed, email contact@2uhub.com with the subject "Copyright notice" and include:

  • What the work is, and that you own it or act for the owner
  • Where it appears on 2UHub (the page address, or the roster name and author)
  • Your name, email address and postal address
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not allowed by the owner, their agent or the law
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner
  • Your signature (typed is fine)

We review notices promptly and remove content that appears to infringe. We may tell the person who shared it. If you shared something that was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can reply with a counter-notice that identifies the content and explains why you have the right to use it, and we will consider restoring it.

Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of people who repeatedly share infringing content.

Anything else

For abuse, offensive content, or personal information shared without permission, use the same email address and tell us where to find it.
