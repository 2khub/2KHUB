Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Who We Are

2UHub (2uhub.com) is an unofficial fan project run by an individual. The person responsible for your personal data is the operator of 2UHub, who you can reach at contact@2uhub.com.

2. What We Collect

On your device

The draft, match card and roster tools save their data in your browser (local storage): your drafts, rosters, settings and your cookie choice. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Accounts

If you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive your email address and the name and avatar of that account, and we store them with a display name you choose in our database (Supabase). We do not receive your password. Your display name is public: it is shown on the rosters you share. If you are signed in, the rosters you save in My rosters are also stored in your account so you can use them on your other devices. They are private to you until you press Share; the ones you share are visible to everyone. When you report a roster, we store which account reported it.

Multiplayer

When you play a multiplayer draft, the room (the names you type, the picks and the chosen roster) is stored in Firebase while the room exists and is removed when the host leaves.

Cookies and similar technologies

The site works without optional cookies. Only if you press "Accept all" in the cookie banner we load:

  • Google AdSense, which shows ads and may use cookies to personalise them. See Google Ads Settings.
  • Microsoft Clarity, which records how visitors use the pages (clicks, scrolling, page views) to help us find problems. It receives your IP address and device details.

You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. Choosing "Only necessary" loads neither of them.

Server logs

Our website host (GitHub Pages) and the services above may log technical data such as your IP address and browser when you visit.

3. Why We Use Your Data

  • To run the Service, keep you signed in and show your display name on rosters you share (needed to provide the Service)
  • To moderate shared rosters, prevent abuse and keep the site secure (our legitimate interest)
  • To show ads and measure how the site is used (only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time)

4. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal information. These providers process data for us or on their own account:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in and shared rosters
  • Google (Firebase): multiplayer draft rooms
  • Google and Discord: only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply
  • Google AdSense and Microsoft Clarity: only after you accept cookies
  • GitHub Pages, cdnjs and jsDelivr: host the site and some script files, so they see your IP address when your browser loads them

Fonts are served from our own site. Some providers are based in the United States and rely on safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

5. How Long We Keep It

  • Account data and rosters saved in your account: until you delete them or your account
  • Multiplayer rooms: until the room ends
  • Your cookie choice and local tool data: in your browser until you clear it
  • Ad and analytics data: as set by Google and Microsoft in their policies

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. From the account menu you can download your data and delete your account, which removes your profile, your shared rosters and your reports. You can also write to contact@2uhub.com. You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the right to know, delete and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; we do not sell it, and "Only necessary" opts you out of advertising cookies.

7. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we post changes on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@2uhub.com.
